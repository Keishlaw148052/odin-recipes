Sourdough Peach Cobbler

Sourdough peach cobbler: Juicy peaches baked beneath buttery sourdough biscuits with a cinnamon-sugar topping. Serves 6.

Ingredients

    Biscuit Dough

  • 210 g all-purpose flour
  • 100 g granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 113 g cold unsalted butter
  • 100 g cold sourdough discard
  • 45 g cold milk, plus extra if needed

    • Peach Filling

  • 1.5 kg fresh peaches
  • 50 g soft brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons finely grated lemon zest
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

    • Topping

  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon cinnamon

Steps

  1. Combine biscuit dry ingredients. Rub in cubed butter until crumbly.
  2. Whisk discard with milk; stir into flour mixture. If dough won’t hold together, add milk a teaspoon at a time.
  3. Cover and refrigerate 1–24 hours.
  4. Heat oven to 350°F (180°C). Cut peaches into 1-inch chunks; toss with all filling ingredients in a 9 × 13-inch baking dish.
  5. Flatten pieces of dough over peaches, leaving gaps.
  6. Combine topping ingredients; sprinkle over dough.
  7. Bake 50–60 minutes, until biscuits are golden and fruit bubbles.
  8. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
