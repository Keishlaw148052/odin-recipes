Apple Pie

A buttery, flaky apple pie filled with tender apples, brown sugar, and warm spices. The filling is cooked beforehand for a thick, juicy center and baked beneath a golden crust.

Ingredients

  • Dough for bottom and top pie crusts, homemade or store-bought
  • 8 cups peeled, sliced tart apples (about 2½ pounds)
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1¼ cups light brown sugar
  • 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cloves
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 2 tablespoons cold water
  • 1 beaten egg, for brushing
  • Optional: granulated sugar for topping

Steps

  1. Slice apples ¼-inch thick. Melt butter over medium heat; add apples and cook 5 minutes.
  2. Add brown sugar, spices, salt, lemon juice, and vanilla. Cook another 10–15 minutes until tender.
  3. Mix cornstarch with cold water; stir into apples until thickened. Cool to room temperature, approximately 1–2 hours.
  4. Line your pie pan with rolled-out dough. Add filling, cover with a lattice crust, and crimp edges.
  5. Refrigerate 20 minutes. Preheat oven to 425°F.
  6. Brush with beaten egg; optionally sprinkle sugar.
  7. Bake 20 minutes, then reduce to 375°F for 30–40 minutes, until golden and bubbling. Shield browning edges with foil.
  8. Cool completely before slicing.
