Banana Bread

A soft, moist banana bread made with ripe bananas and sour cream, with optional chocolate chips and walnuts for extra sweetness and crunch. Perfect for breakfast or a sweet snack.

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, melted
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Optional: ½ cup chopped walnuts or chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9 × 5-inch loaf pan and line with parchment, leaving overhang for lifting.
  2. Mash 2 bananas, keeping some small lumps. Thinly slice the remaining banana.
  3. Stir melted butter and sugar together. Add eggs and vanilla; mix until smooth.
  4. Stir in sour cream, mashed bananas, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gently mix in flour just until combined.
  6. Fold in sliced banana, chocolate chips, and walnuts, if using.
  7. Pour into the pan. Bake 50–60 minutes, until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Cool in the pan for 15 minutes, then lift out and transfer to a wire rack.
  9. Yield: About 8 servings.
